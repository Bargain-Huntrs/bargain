## BOTTLENECKS

- **`arbitrage_deals` missing composite index** — public feed filters `is_profitable AND status='active' AND historical_avg NOT NULL AND buy_price <= historical_avg*0.8` then `ORDER BY net_profit DESC LIMIT 600` (already bounded — was full-table 86s). No index on `status`, `is_profitable`, or `net_profit` (only asin/retailer/niche/detected_at/archived_at indexed). ~0.9s today at ~2k active rows; will degrade linearly. Recommend alembic migration: `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY ix_arb_deals_public_feed ON arbitrage_deals (status, is_profitable) WHERE status='active' AND is_profitable` (+ net_profit DESC for the sort). Flagged, not shipped (prod DDL on shared Supabase).
- **No fetch timeouts in `lib/api.ts` — FIXED.** `fetchPublic`/`fetchWithAuth` + 14 raw `fetch(API_URL…)` callers had no timeout — a Render cold start left skeletons spinning forever (the login-hang fix added fetchWithTimeout to authService but not api.ts). New `apiFetch` wrapper (AbortSignal.timeout 30s) now covers all API_URL calls in the file; errors surface as normal error states.
- Client-side post-filters on the deals feed (junk-title/aggregator/language/dedupe) run in Python over a 600-row window — fine now; if the feed grows, push dedupe into SQL.
- Images: all `<img>` raw (no next/image in use) → `images.unoptimized` question is moot on the worker; lazy-loading already on deal grids per prior audit.
